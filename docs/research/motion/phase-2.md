# Phase 2 motion audit (Section 5.8, MV-001 to MV-008)

Audit of the Phase 2 client at `551ee1da` (package branch `claude/wp-34-acceptance`, worktree `.claude/worktrees/wp-34`). Reviewer: interface reviewer, WP-34 T2.
The reviewer built none of this code. No application code was changed. All data is synthetic (CON-003, Section 5.8). WP-17 D-13 and D-14 and WP-34 D-5 applied from the start (R-066): under reduced motion an instant change in place is the specified form and is never a finding.

**Verdict for acceptance criterion 17 (phase-2-plan Section 5): NOT MET as worded; no finding reaches the D-5 block clause.**

- Sentence 1 holds. Every motion role Phase 2 uses (session tab open, menu and popover, chart update, loading, modal, readings) has a measured reduced-motion form (MOT-040, MOT-041): fade only or instant, scale 1 in every frame.
- Sentence 2 fails on the letter: 3 open "layout jump" findings (M-1 32 px, M-2 64 px, 24 px and 12 px, M-3 2.1 px). No "wrong motion" is open. The 4 findings of other MV-002 classes (S-1 to S-4: loader without a handoff, dead swap, missing feedback) are listed separately.
- Sentence 3 holds in the browser: nothing animates, filters or covers a live display (MAT-008). C-4 (the desktop web view and `overlayOpen`) needs the operator.
- The largest step is 64 px, under the 100 px clause of D-5. By D-5 every jump goes to issue #1 as a could and the criterion is met with the open items named. Fix M-2 first: it moves a destructive Stop button under the pointer.

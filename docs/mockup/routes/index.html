Routes

The routes of the hub, as in Nginx Proxy Manager, with the hub as the proxy (Sections 12 and 16 of hosted-applications-stage-2.md). Routes is a section of Settings, in the group Hub: the routes belong to the hub and not to one device, they are set up rarely, and a problem of a route comes to you as a check and a notification (CHK-007, NOT-006), so the navigation bar of NAV-001 does not change. The page of an application proposes its route (Frame 6). The API of today (task H21) has web routes only: add, check, remove, with the proof and the certificate. No route serves yet: the certificate issuer and the consent at the machine are not built. A part marked Planned needs task H40 or a later task: redirects, streams, the access of a route and the step that makes it public.
